METHOD 1: INSTANT IN-APP DELETION (RECOMMENDED)

Delete Directly Inside the App

If you have the Ahwale Gujranwala app installed on your device, you can delete your account immediately:

  1. Open the Ahwale Gujranwala app.
  2. Tap your profile icon at the top right of the screen.
  3. Tap Settings (ترتیبات).
  4. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account (اکاؤنٹ حذف کریں)".
  5. Confirm your choice. Your account, session token, phone number, and personal listings will be deleted instantly.
