1. Overview & Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how Ahwale Gujranwala ("we", "our", or "the platform") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you use our mobile application and web portal available at https://ahwalegujranwala.com. We take data protection seriously and adhere to Google Play Developer policies regarding user data safety.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your Pakistani mobile number, full name, and selected tehsil (Gujranwala City, Kamoke, Nowshera Virkan, Qila Didar Singh, Wazirabad, or Cantt). Your password is encrypted and securely stored using industry-standard bcrypt hashing.
  • User-Generated Content: Listing titles, descriptions, prices, photos, and contact phone/WhatsApp numbers you voluntarily submit when posting a classified ad, service listing, or community announcement.
  • Location Data (Optional): If you grant permission, the app accesses your device's approximate or precise GPS coordinates solely to power the "Nearby Search" feature (calculating distances to nearby shops and service providers within 2 km, 5 km, 10 km, or 20 km). Location data is processed dynamically in memory and is never sold or used for background ad tracking.
  • Profile Photos: If you upload a profile avatar or photos for your ads, they are stored on secure cloud hosting.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide peer-to-peer community connectivity (e.g., enabling interested buyers to contact you directly via phone or WhatsApp).
  • To moderate submitted listings and announcements to prevent fraud, spam, or abusive content.
  • To calculate distances for local neighborhood searches.
  • To maintain app security, prevent bot traffic, and enforce platform integrity.

4. User Content Moderation & Reporting

All public listings, service advertisements, and community notices undergo administrative moderation before becoming visible to the community. Furthermore, users can report any offensive, misleading, or fraudulent listing or bad AI response directly using the in-app "Report" button. Our moderation team reviews reported items within 24 hours.

5. AI Assistant Safety (Google Gemini & Local Engine)

Our platform includes a built-in community assistant powered by automated local knowledge and the Google Gemini API. The assistant operates via a secure server proxy with no personal keys or private user messages exposed. AI responses are automated and may contain inaccuracies. Users can report any harmful or inaccurate AI answer using the in-chat "Report answer" feature.

6. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

We believe in full user control over personal data. You have the right to permanently delete your account and associated personal information at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Tap your profile icon > Settings > "Delete Account (اکاؤنٹ حذف کریں)". This immediately deletes your profile, removes your phone number, and anonymizes your listings.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the app, you can request permanent deletion on our web page at https://ahwalegujranwala.com/delete-account.html or by emailing us at support@ahwalegujranwala.com with your registered phone number. Requests are processed within 48 hours.

7. Children's Privacy

Our platform is intended for general audiences aged 13 and above (or 18 and above for commercial trading). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
